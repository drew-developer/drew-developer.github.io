Nonogram: Premium Pixel Logic ("the App") is a logic puzzle application developed by Drew Developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Our primary philosophy is Privacy Extremism. We aim to collect the absolute minimum amount of data required to provide a stable and enjoyable experience.

A. Analytics Data

If you have opted-in to "Usage Analytics" in the app settings, we use Firebase Analytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us balance puzzle difficulty and improve the user interface. Data collected includes:

  • Anonymous event data (e.g., puzzles completed, features used, app sessions).
  • Device information (e.g., model, OS version, regional settings).

This data is strictly anonymous. It contains no Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, or precise location.

B. Crash Reporting

To ensure app stability, we use Firebase Crashlytics. This collects technical logs (stack traces) when the app crashes or encounters an error. This information is used solely for bug fixing.

C. Local Storage

The App stores your progress, settings, and earned coins locally on your device via DataStore and Room SQL Database. This data is not transferred to any external servers unless you perform a manual backup via system tools.

2. Advertising (Rewarded Ads)

The App uses Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads (e.g., for claiming daily bonuses).

  • AdMob may use advertising IDs (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • You can control ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. User Control & Choices

We believe you should have total control over your data:

  • Analytics Opt-Out: You can enable or disable analytics tracking at any time via Settings > Usage Analytics.
  • Data Deletion: Since we do not store your data on our own servers (it is local or anonymous), you can delete your local usage data by clearing the App's cache/storage in your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience and is rated Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • AdMob
