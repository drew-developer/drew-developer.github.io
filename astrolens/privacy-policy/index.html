Privacy Policy

AstroLens is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our application.

1. Local-Only Processing

AstroLens is designed with a Privacy-First philosophy. All data processing—including celestial calculations, image stacking, and AI-based star identification—happens entirely on your device.

We do not operate any backend servers, and your data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose to share it via the Android system share menu.

2. Information We Use

A. Location Data
  • Purpose: To calculate accurate azimuth and elevation coordinates for celestial objects based on your current position on Earth.
  • Storage: Your location data is used only during the active session. It is never stored permanently or transmitted to any third party.
B. Camera Data
  • Purpose: To provide the Augmented Reality (AR) sky overlay and to capture photos of the night sky.
  • Processing: Video frames from your camera are processed in real-time for star detection and identification using local AI models. No camera data is uploaded to the cloud.
C. Storage Access
  • Purpose: To save your captured astrophotography images directly to your device's gallery (MediaStore).

3. Third-Party Services

AstroLens does not include any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising platforms. There are no "telemetry" or "usage reporting" features.

4. Artificial Intelligence

All AI-based features, such as star identification and constellation mapping, are powered by local LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite) models. No image data is sent to external AI providers or servers.

5. Security

Because all your data remains on your device, the security of your information is tied to the security of your Android device. We recommend using standard device security measures to protect your photos.
