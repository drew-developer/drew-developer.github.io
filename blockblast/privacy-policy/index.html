Privacy Policy for BlockBlast: Pro Edition

Last Updated: February 6, 2026

BlockBlast: Pro Edition ("we", "our", or "the Game") is designed with user privacy as a priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • Gameplay Data: All game progress, high scores, levels, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device using your device's internal storage. This data is never sent to our servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for, or have access to, your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal identifiers.

2. Permissions

The Game requests the following permissions for the sole purpose of enhancing your gameplay experience:

  • Vibrate (Haptic Feedback): Used to provide tactile feedback during block placement, line clears, and game events.
  • Storage Access: Used strictly to save and load your high scores and game settings locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

BlockBlast: Pro Edition is a minimalist experience.

  • We do not use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Flurry).
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks that track user behavior.
  • We do not integrate with social media platforms.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, the Game is inherently safe for children. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

5. Security

Your game data is as secure as your mobile device. Since we do not transmit data over the internet, your information cannot be intercepted from our servers.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the official store listing.
