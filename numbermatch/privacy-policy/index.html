Privacy Policy for Number Match

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Number Match ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. OUR PRIVACY PHILOSOPHY 🛡️

At Number Match, we believe in Privacy Extremism. We built this game so you can enjoy a pure logic puzzle experience without worrying about your data being tracked, sold, or analyzed.

2. NO DATA COLLECTION

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to play.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not use any third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) to track your in-game behavior.

3. LOCAL DATA STORAGE

All game progress, including your high scores, unlocked achievements, and settings (such as your chosen neon theme), is stored locally on your device using an internal database.

  • This data does not leave your device.
  • If you delete the app, this local data will be removed.

4. PERMISSIONS

Number Match requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core gameplay features:

  • Vibration: To provide tactile (haptic) feedback during matches and UI interactions.
  • Local Storage: Standard access to save your game state locally.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Number Match is a standalone experience.

  • No Ads: We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • No Social Logins: We do not use Facebook, Google, or other social login providers.
  • No External Links: Aside from standard system sharing intents (which you control), the app does not link to external tracking sites.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Because we do not collect any personal information, Number Match is safe for users of all ages and is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. OFFLINE FUNCTIONALITY

The game is fully functional offline. An internet connection is not required to play, and the app does not attempt to connect to any servers while you play.
