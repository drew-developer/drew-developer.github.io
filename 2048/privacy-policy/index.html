100% Private No Ads Offline First

01Absolute Privacy Commitment

The App is designed with a "Privacy First" philosophy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or device-identifying information.

02Data Collection and Usage

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers.
  • Usage Data: We do not track how you use the App, how long you play, or which features you use.
  • Device Data: We do not collect technical information about your device, such as IP addresses or hardware IDs.

03Local Data Storage

All progress within the App, including your high scores, current game state, settings, and "Hall of Fame" records, is stored locally on your device.

  • This data is managed through Android's Jetpack DataStore.
  • This data is never transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • You can delete all App-related data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing storage in device settings.

04Permissions

The App requests only essential permissions:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. This can be toggled in settings.

The App does not require or use Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET).

05Third-Party Services

Transparency is key. The App does not include:

  • Third-party advertising.
  • Third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics).
  • Tracking-enabled social media buttons.

06Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, the App is inherently safe for users of all ages, including children.
